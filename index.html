Safety brief

Two minutes to read. Non-negotiables in red.

The stairs are the main risk
Steep ascent on the Summit Steps AND descent on the Rengas Path. Use the guide ropes where provided, watch your footing, and go slow on the way down — most twisted ankles happen descending, not climbing. Trekking pole welcome.
Heat management
We pace to the slowest member — always. Regular water sips, not gulps. Giddiness, nausea or chills = stop and tell your buddy immediately. Nobody pushes into the red for timing's sake.
Buddy system + headcounts
Pair up at the gathering point. You are responsible for your buddy. Headcounts at the foot of the Summit Steps, the summit, Wallace Centre and back at Car Park A. Mission Success. Safety Always.
Monkeys
Long-tailed macaques roam the reserve. Food stays sealed and kept away from them — out of sight. No feeding, no eye contact, no snacking on-trail. They're bold, not dangerous — unless you're holding food.
Stay on marked trails
Do NOT enter the mountain-bike trail — collision risk and a $200 fine. Follow NParks signage only. If separated, stop where you are and call your buddy and send a text in our telegram group.
Wet weather / lightning
Thunder = off the exposed summit and stairs, shelter at the trail huts or Wallace Centre. Go/no-go call by 0700 on the morning via the group chat. Backup plan: flat stroll to the Singapore Quarry viewing platform from Car Park A, or straight to Rail Mall brunch.
Graceful opt-out
Not feeling the stairs on the day? Take the flat track from Car Park A to the Singapore Quarry viewing platform instead (~1 h easy stroll) and rejoin us at The Rail Mall for lunch. No one gets forced up, no one walks alone.
Emergency info
Rally point: Car Park A. Nearest hospital: Ng Teng Fong General, ~10 min drive. Emergency: 995. NParks helpline: 1800-471-7300.
