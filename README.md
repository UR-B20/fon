# fon

Event pages served via GitHub Pages.

- `/` — Ex Summit Sisters, Female Owls Quarterly Gathering (12 Jul).
- `/15c4i/` — 15 C4I Bn 6th Anniversary: Launch of Panthera HQ ceremony + evening celebration (15 Sep).
- `/15c4i/celebration.html` — 15 C4I Bn 6th Anniversary: evening celebration only.

The 15 C4I pages load two images from the `15c4i/` folder. Until they are added, a drawn crest is shown and the satellite photo section is hidden:

- `15c4i/logo.png` — battalion crest.
- `15c4i/map.jpg` — annotated satellite view (Carpark B and walk up to Blk 7).
