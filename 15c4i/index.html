Getting there

Drive in via the Clementi Camp guard room off Clementi Ave 6, park at Carpark B, then walk up the slope to Blk 7 (Old K9 Hill). Tap a leg pill or a dot for details.

Clementi Camp guard room entry · Clementi Ave 6

Enter the camp via the guard room on Clementi Ave 6. Follow the internal road north, then turn left towards Carpark B.

Event advisories

Please take note of the following for the duration of the event.

Photography and videography
Strictly not permitted. Authorised photographers will be on site to cover the event.
Restricted areas
Please remain within the event location at all times. Movement to other parts of the camp is not permitted.
Green Zone
Blk 7 is a Green Zone. Green Zone rules apply for the duration of the event.
Toilets
Male and female toilets are located on the 1st storey of Blk 7 HQ.
